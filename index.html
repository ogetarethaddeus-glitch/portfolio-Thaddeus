02 — Flagship project
GROUP PROJECT · GROUP 9

PSO-based morphing wing optimization for a ScanEagle UAV proxy

A trailing-edge morphing mechanism for the SD7062 airfoil, optimized using particle swarm optimization coupled to XFOIL across three flight regimes, then realized as a physical cross-axis compliant flexure mechanism and validated under finite element analysis.

Airfoil
SD7062
Optimizer
PSO + XFOIL
Flight regimes

Mechanism material
Nylon 6/6
Peak von Mises stress
27.952 MPa
Factor of safety

Deployment was handled by an Arduino-driven servo system using a precomputed lookup table rather than live PSO — trading online optimization for deterministic, low-latency actuation suited to flight hardware.
